My research sits at the intersection of intelligent systems, explainability, and practical real-world constraints. My interests span Bangla NLP, social-media safety, retrieval-augmented generation, intelligent agents, computer vision, trustworthy and explainable AI, federated learning, and AI for cybersecurity and network security.

Through the IEEE GUB Student Branch, I contribute to technical programs and research-focused community activity, and I welcome collaboration with students, researchers, and practitioners working on related problems.
