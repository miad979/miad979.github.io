  <h3 style="margin: 0 0 8px 0; font-size: 20px; color: var(--text-main);">AI-Driven Self-Healing Networks: Integrating Deep Reinforcement Learning in Next-Gen Topologies</h3>
  
  <div class="pub-authors">
    <strong>M. M. I. Nizzan</strong>
  </div>
  
  <div class="pub-venue">
    Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Green University of Bangladesh
  </div>

  <p style="font-size: 14px; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 15px;">
    <strong>Abstract:</strong> Modern high-throughput network engineering demands autonomous fault management. This paper details a novel architecture combining Deep Learning predictive models with Reinforcement Learning loops ($Q$-learning frameworks) to construct a self-healing software-defined network (SDN). The engine actively maps traffic metrics, predicts unexpected structural bottlenecks, isolates malicious nodes, and automatically recalculates optimal, self-healing routing pathways without human intervention.
  </p>
</div>
